AgriGuard — Pest & Disease Monitoring for Banana Lakatan Farming

01 · Problem
Manual agricultural inspection is slow, inconsistent, and dependent on individual expertise. Early pest and disease detection in banana Lakatan farming is critical to yield — but human inspectors cannot scale across large plantations.
02 · Solution
An integrated monitoring system combining CNN-based image classification, computer vision, embedded sensors, and an automated spraying mechanism — turning visual detection into a physical response without manual intervention.
03 · My Engineering Contribution
  • Trained and evaluated a CNN image-classification model for banana pest and disease detection.
  • Experimented with ViT and YOLO-based vision approaches for detection robustness.
  • Integrated sensors and actuators with Arduino and Raspberry Pi.
  • Built the hardware-to-software communication layer and automated spraying control.
  • Developed the monitoring dashboard and database integration.

PlantDoc — Crop-Agnostic Plant Disease Detection

01 · Problem
Most plant-disease models are single-crop. Farmers with mixed crops need a tool that can diagnose any leaf — without retraining a model for every species.
02 · Solution
A deep-learning system capable of detecting 33 disease types across multiple plants. Users upload any leaf image and receive a diagnosis with confidence scores. CNN-based classification with transfer learning, and exploration of vision-transformer approaches for complex real-world leaf images.
03 · My Engineering Contribution
  • Built the image pipeline (OpenCV) for real-world leaf normalization.
  • Applied transfer learning to a pre-trained CNN backbone.
  • Developed a Flask inference server and upload interface.
  • Evaluated model accuracy and failure modes on complex leaf images.

Stock Management & Sales System for an Electronics Shop

01 · Problem
A local electronics and sensors shop tracked inventory manually. Stockouts, mismatched sales records, and lack of role separation were causing losses and slow customer service.
02 · Solution
A complete inventory and point-of-sale system with real-time stock updates, low-stock alerts, sales reports, receipt generation, and role-based access for admin and cashier users.
03 · My Engineering Contribution
  • Designed the relational database schema for products, categories, sales, and users.
  • Built the Laravel backend with role-based access control.
  • Implemented real-time stock deduction and low-stock alert logic.
  • Developed sales reporting and receipt generation.
