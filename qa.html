QA Evaluation · Attendance & HR Analytics Localhost Only

INSIGHTATTEND: Employee Attendance Management System with Facial Recognition and Descriptive Analytics

A school-side employee attendance system using facial recognition and descriptive analytics. Evaluated for records integrity, authentication policy, and system architecture.

Scope System Design · Security & Policy · Authentication · Records Management · HR Workflow
System Design (Positive) The overall system design is good and well-aligned with the project's goal as a records system. The architecture and data flow are solid. The concern is not the design itself, but how tightly it is locked down for security and policy compliance.
Security & Policy Because this is a records-heavy school system, it must be made highly secure by default. Recommended enforcing strict access control, encrypting records at rest and in transit, logging every attendance and record change, and clearly separating the roles of employees, HR, and admins.
Authentication & Forgot Password The system should not be open to everyone, and the forgot-password flow must not be a free self-service reset. Because it is deployed in a school, account recovery should go through HR confirmation and approval before a reset is granted. HR verifies the employee's identity and authorizes the reset, which prevents unauthorized account takeover on a system that holds sensitive attendance records.
Deactivation for Leave (ML / Maternity) Add an account deactivation feature for employees on ML (Maternity Leave) or any other approved leave. Their accounts should be deactivated while they are on leave and automatically reactivated, or cleanly restored, when they return. This preserves their records and history without allowing attendance activity during the leave period.
